About me

Quality is a habit,
not a final check.

I am a Quality Control Inspector at Spain Bangladesh Agro Industry Ltd., working across the manufacturing process for canned sweet corn, pineapple and mango products.

My work combines careful inspection with a practical understanding of food safety systems. I monitor critical control points, verify specifications, inspect can seams, check raw materials and finished products, and maintain clear quality records.

I enjoy turning observations into improvements that production teams can act on. My goal is simple: make quality standards clear, repeatable and dependable.
