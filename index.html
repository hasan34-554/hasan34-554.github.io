About Me

I am a Quality Control Inspector at Spain Bangladesh Agro Industries Ltd., responsible for product quality, food safety and compliance throughout the manufacturing process of canned food products, particularly sweet corn, pineapple and mango.

I believe quality is not only about meeting specifications but also about building consumer trust. I perform inspections across the production line, monitor critical control points, verify product specifications, conduct can seam inspections, check raw materials and finished products, and maintain quality records.

I work closely with production teams to identify quality issues, apply corrective actions and support continuous improvement. I'm always open to connecting with professionals and researchers who share an interest in food quality, food safety and manufacturing excellence.
